# Attendance, Assessment, and Grading

Students are expected to attend at least 80% of scheduled sessions in courses that record attendance. A course syllabus may set a higher threshold when required by accreditation, laboratory safety, or clinical practice, but it may not set a lower threshold. Three consecutive unexplained absences trigger an academic-support alert; the alert itself is not a disciplinary finding.

An absence is excused only after the student submits acceptable documentation through the portal within five business days of returning. An excused absence allows a reasonable alternative for participation work, but it does not automatically cancel a learning outcome or guarantee the same assessment format. Students must still meet essential laboratory, clinical, or placement requirements.

Northstar uses letter grades `A`, `B`, `C`, `D`, and `F`, with plus and minus modifiers where a course syllabus permits them. A final course grade must follow the assessment weights published by the end of the first teaching week. An instructor may correct a calculation or data-entry error, but may not create a new assessment after final grades are published solely to raise one student's grade.

An `I` incomplete grade may be granted when at least 70% of assessed work is complete, the student was passing before an unexpected documented event, and the remaining work can be completed independently. The student and instructor must sign an incomplete plan. The default deadline is the end of the next regular term. If no grade is submitted by that deadline, the `I` converts to `F` unless an approved extension exists.

A disagreement about academic judgement is handled first with the instructor. Alleged calculation error, procedural unfairness, discrimination, or inconsistency with the published syllabus may proceed to the formal grade-appeal route in `08_student_support_and_appeals.md`.
