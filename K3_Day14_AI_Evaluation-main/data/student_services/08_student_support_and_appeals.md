# Student Support, Complaints, and Appeals

The Student Success Centre provides study-skills coaching, tutoring referrals, and non-clinical wellbeing navigation. Accessibility Services coordinates reasonable academic adjustments after reviewing documentation. Adjustments are not retroactive unless the office confirms that an exceptional barrier prevented earlier registration. Neither office changes grades or waives essential programme outcomes.

A service complaint concerns delay, communication, or conduct by an administrative unit. The student should first contact the unit and allow five business days for a response. If unresolved, a formal complaint may be filed within 20 business days of the event. Complaints involving discrimination, harassment, immediate safety, or suspected fraud may be escalated directly without completing the informal step.

A grade appeal is different from a service complaint. The student must first request clarification from the instructor within five business days after the final grade is published. A formal grade appeal must be filed within ten business days after publication and must identify at least one permitted ground: calculation error, material departure from the published syllabus, procedural unfairness, or prohibited discrimination. Disagreement with academic judgement alone is not a permitted ground.

The department chair reviews a grade appeal first. A student may appeal the chair's outcome to the Academic Review Panel within five business days only when there is new material evidence or a documented procedural error in the first review. The Panel's decision is final within the university process.

Scholarship decisions use the same ten-business-day filing window but go to the Financial Aid Review Committee. Registration and fee exceptions go to the Registrar or Student Accounts, respectively. An appeal pauses neither a payment deadline nor an academic deadline unless the responsible office grants a written temporary hold.

Students should submit cases through the portal and keep the case number. Sensitive evidence must not be sent through public chat or included in a general support ticket.
