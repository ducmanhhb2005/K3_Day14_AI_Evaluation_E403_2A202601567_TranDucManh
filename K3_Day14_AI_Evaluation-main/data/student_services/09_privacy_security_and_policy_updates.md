# Privacy, Account Security, and Policy Updates

Northstar uses the student portal and university email for official account communication. Multi-factor authentication is required for the portal. Staff will never request a password or one-time authentication code. A student who suspects account compromise should change the password from a trusted device, revoke active sessions, and contact the IT Service Desk. Suspected payment fraud should also be reported to Student Accounts and the student's payment provider.

The university may discuss a student's record only with the student or a person who has a valid, recorded authorization. A parent or sponsor who pays tuition does not automatically receive academic or conduct information. Students may download their own enrolment and billing records from the portal. Requests to correct inaccurate personal data go to the Records Office; requests to change an academic decision must use the relevant appeal process.

Policy documents display a version and effective date. The policy in force on the triggering event date controls unless a newer policy explicitly states that it applies retroactively. A newer publication does not silently rewrite an earlier transaction. When the event spans multiple dates, use the date identified by the policy: for registration it is the registration action date, for tuition refunds it is the recorded drop or withdrawal date, and for scholarship review it is the end of the reviewed term.

Registration Policy version 1.0, effective before August 1, 2026, allowed late adds for seven calendar days after standard add/drop and charged USD 25. Version 2.0, effective August 1, 2026, allows late adds only through census and charges USD 40 per course. A late-add request made on or after August 1, 2026 follows version 2.0 even if the student first discussed the request in July.

Security incidents involving immediate unauthorized disclosure are escalated to the Privacy Officer. Routine access questions go first to the IT Service Desk. Students should include a case number and event time, but should not copy passwords, authentication codes, full card numbers, or unrelated personal records into a ticket.
